Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Purpose

Fuji Restic Backup is a personal backup application used solely by its owner to store encrypted backups in the owner's Google Drive account.

Google Drive Access

The application uses Google Drive API authorization to create, read, and manage backup files created by the application.

Data Handling

Backup contents are encrypted locally using restic before being uploaded to Google Drive. The application does not sell or share user data with third parties.

Data Retention

Backup files remain in the owner's Google Drive until removed according to the owner's backup retention settings.

Access and Revocation

The owner can revoke the application's Google Drive access through their Google Account security settings.
